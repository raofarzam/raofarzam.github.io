The goal

AirSupply manufactures commercial kitchen ventilation equipment. Every department, from purchasing to HR, works inside one in-house ERP system. The business needed someone who could build new features, design forms people actually find easy to use, and fix problems quickly when something breaks in the middle of a working day.

What I built

I work across the whole platform as part of a small in-house team:

  • Designed and built the Operations screens: Task Setup, Task Manager and Meeting Manager, used to assign and track work across departments.
  • Worked on core business forms including Proforma Invoice, Purchase and Requisition.
  • First line of support across 12 modules: administration, master data, financials, purchases, sales, banking, warehousing, manufacturing, import/export, adjustments, operations and HR & payroll.
  • Alongside development, I look after the company’s IT: network, Windows systems, Microsoft 365 and day-to-day technical support.

Result

The screens I built are used every day across the company. Delivering a major module on my own led to my promotion. Screens shown here come from the live system, with customer and business data blurred.
