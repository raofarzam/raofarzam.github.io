Velocity Cycles

A complete bicycle e-commerce store with road, mountain, electric and kids’ bikes, a full shopping flow and its own admin panel.

The goal

The goal was a store that feels like a real shop, not a template: strong product photography, easy browsing by bike type, and an admin side where products, orders and customers can be managed without touching code.

What I built

  • Shop by category, with filters and search.
  • Product pages, cart, wishlist, checkout and order confirmation.
  • Customer sign-up and login.
  • An admin panel with dashboard, products, add and edit product, orders, customers, categories and settings.
  • Image uploads handled through Cloudinary.

Result

A working demo you can try: browse, add to cart and check out. Open the live store.
