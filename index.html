About

Two skills most people hire separately.

I’m Rao Farzam Ahmed. For three years I’ve worked on both sides of the web: building the software businesses run on, and getting websites found on Google.

Today I’m a Software Developer at AirSupply, a manufacturing company in Karachi, where I work on the company’s in-house ERP platform: an integrated system covering administration, purchasing, sales, banking, warehousing, import/export and HR. I develop and troubleshoot modules with ASP.NET, VB.NET and React.js, and I designed and built operations screens such as Task Setup, Task Manager and Meeting Manager. Independently delivering a major module earned me a promotion.

Before that, I spent two years as an SEO Specialist at LegitXquad, ranking client websites in the UK, US and UAE. Off-page SEO and link building are my strongest skills, and I once ranked a US client’s target keyword in just 15 days.

Alongside my job, I build WordPress and e-commerce websites and write SEO content for clients. Working inside a real business has taught me to build things people actually use every day, and to care about results, not just code.

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely, and my SEO work has been for clients in the UK, US and UAE. We can talk on WhatsApp, email or a video call at a time that suits your time zone.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size. A small business website usually takes one to three weeks, an online store longer. You’ll get a clear timeline after our first conversation.

Can you work on my existing website?

Yes. I can improve its design, speed or SEO, or add new pages and features, whether it’s WordPress or custom-built.

Can you guarantee the #1 spot on Google?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. What I can promise is honest SEO work: the right keywords, a well-built site and quality links, with clear reports so you can see progress.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

If you want to, yes. WordPress sites are easy to edit and I’ll show you how. For custom sites I can build a simple admin area or handle updates for you.
